Privacy Policy

Anti Anti-Debug — Chrome Extension

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Summary

Anti Anti-Debug does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. The extension runs entirely inside your browser and sends nothing to us or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. Specifically, the extension does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, address)
  • Browsing history or the URLs you visit
  • Page content, form input, or credentials
  • Analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or advertising identifiers

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — remembers a single on/off switch locally in your browser. It never leaves your device.
  • scripting — injects a small script into the page you are viewing so anti-debugging checks can be neutralized.
  • tabs — reads the active tab so the extension knows where to apply its effect and can reload that tab when you toggle a switch.
  • debugger — used only when you explicitly enable “Deep mode”; it attaches to the current tab to skip debugger pauses and is detached as soon as you switch tabs or turn it off.
  • http://*/* and https://*/* — the extension works on regular web pages, so it needs access to the pages you open. It performs no network requests of its own.

All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is uploaded, logged remotely, or shared.

Third parties

The extension contains an optional sponsorship link and QR code that open a PayPal page in a new tab. If you choose to use it, your interaction is handled entirely by PayPal under PayPal’s privacy policy. The extension itself does not process payments or receive your payment details.

Children’s privacy

The extension is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

隱私政策

Anti Anti-Debug — Chrome 擴充功能

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日

摘要

本擴充功能不收集、不儲存、不傳輸、也不販售任何個人資料。所有處理 都在你的瀏覽器本機完成，不會向我們或任何第三方傳送任何資料。

我們收集的資料

沒有。具體而言，本擴充功能不收集：

  • 可識別個人身分的資訊（姓名、Email、地址）
  • 瀏覽紀錄或你造訪的網址
  • 網頁內容、表單輸入或帳密資料
  • 分析、遙測、當機報告或廣告識別碼

權限用途說明

  • storage：在本機記住一個開關狀態，不會離開你的裝置。
  • scripting：對你正在瀏覽的頁面注入一小段腳本，用來中和反調試偵測。
  • tabs：讀取目前分頁，以決定作用位置，並在你切換開關時重新載入該分頁。
  • debugger：僅在你主動開啟「深度模式」時使用，會附加到目前分頁以略過 debugger 暫停；切換分頁或關閉時會立即卸離。
  • http://*/* 與 https://*/*：本擴充功能運作於一般網頁，因此需要存取你開啟的頁面；它本身不會發出任何網路請求。

第三方

擴充功能內含一個選用的贊助連結與二維碼，會以新分頁開啟 PayPal 頁面。 若你選擇使用，該互動完全由 PayPal 依其 隱私政策 處理。本擴充功能本身不處理付款，也不會取得你的付款資訊。

兒童隱私

本擴充功能為開發者工具，並非針對兒童設計。我們不會刻意收集任何人 （包含兒童）的資訊。
